# Nutrition guidance

Start `AgBot - Nutrition - <TODAY>`. Apply the shared coaching policy and the user's
actual goals, restrictions and agreed targets. Show estimated calorie/protein
targets, reported intake today and remaining when the supplied data supports it.
Separate consumed from planned meals and uncertainty in portion estimates.
Do not count partial-day expenditure as an intake allowance or equate fat burned
in exercise with body-fat loss. Offer practical sustainable food choices.
Do not prescribe medical diets; respect professional guidance.
